What we learned

Timing kills intent

Verification before team creation asks for trust before delivering value.

Choice reads as obligation

Too many paths paralyze the user's forward momentum.

Aadhaar mistrust is real

Verification feels like surveillance without clear value.

I also ran 15 to 16 interviews to understand which documents users actually carry and in what order.

The Pivot

From collecting documents → protecting user momentum

Final Solution

Retime the ask

Move KYC to after team creation — when intent is real, not assumed.

1 - Browse & select contest commit intent first
2 - Create team KYC triggered post-save
3 - KYC + Location (grouped) all steps at once
4 - Join contest & pay final transactional step

Improved top-of-funnel cash conversion by 30%

Placing KYC immediately after team creation improved top-of-funnel cash conversion by 30%, without negatively impacting paid contest joins.

User Experience Strategy

Reduce choice, route on failure

Rather than presenting all options upfront, the flow surfaces the highest-SR path first and routes intelligently on failure — using backend success rates, not guesswork.

trending_up

Highest success-rate path surfaced first

Prioritizing the primary flow based on success rate of the instruments.

alt_route

Failure triggers smart fallback, not restart

Instead of blocking the user, the system intelligently shifts to the next viable verification path.

auto_fix_high

Progress preserved across attempts

State management ensures that shared metadata is carried over between primary and fallback routes.

Reflection

Earned lessons

visibility

Show and tell beats tell alone

The 'more steps = more friction' pushback only resolved when I mapped the flow visually — showing the click count staying the same.

architecture

Design work can be made obsolete by external factors

Two flows — the TN location design and the full PAN-at-KYC flow — didn't ship as intended due to RMG ban and regulatory changes.

insights

Track the 28% who still fail

55% → 72% real progress. But 28% still hit a wall. We didn't instrument follow-through on those users post-launch.
