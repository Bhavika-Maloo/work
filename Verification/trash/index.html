Why This Matters
Fantasy sports users are highly invested and time-sensitive, often joining contests just before matches. They expect instant withdrawals after wins, and any verification delays can lead to immediate churn.
Design Strategy

Principles That Guided the Redesign

Five design principles emerged from our research to transform the verification experience

  1. Intuitive
  2. Visual-first
  3. Progressive
  4. Scalable

The Regulatory Reality

In regulated industries, design flexibility isn't nice-to-have — it's survival. Our component-based approach let us pivot quickly while maintaining user experience quality, proving that good design systems are business resilience tools.
