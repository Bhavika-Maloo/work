02 — Context

Why this was hard

Four constraints compounded each other. Understanding all four shaped every design decision.

Infrastructure

Low Aadhaar-mobile linkage

Most users hadn't linked mobile to Aadhaar — ruling out OTP and forcing document fallbacks.

Regulatory

State-level geo-gating

AP, Telangana, TN and others required location checks at every contest entry — not once at reg.

Timing

IPL rush

Drop-off peaks when intent is highest. At 7:25pm on match day, patience is zero.

Complexity

5 docs, 5 paths, 0 guidance

Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, DL — each with different backends and validation flows.

07 — Reflection

Earned lessons

→

Show and tell beats tell alone

The "more steps = more friction" pushback only resolved when I mapped the flow visually — showing the click count staying the same. Not everyone has strong visualisation. That's not a gap to judge; it's a design problem to solve.

!

Design work can be made obsolete by things outside design

Two flows — the TN location design and the full PAN-at-KYC flow — didn't ship as intended. One due to an RMG ban, one to a regulatory change that never reached the brief. The work wasn't wrong. The ground moved. Learning to design resilient handoffs matters as much as the design itself.

→

Track the 28% who still fail

55% → 72% is real progress. But 28% still hit a wall. We didn't instrument follow-through on those users post-launch. That 28% is the next design problem — probably several problems in a trenchcoat.
